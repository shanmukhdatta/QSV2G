- `final_validation_submitted.zip`: final 38-case validation results.
- `full_protocol_openssl_reported.zip`: full-protocol archive with an OpenSSL ML-KEM/ML-DSA backend reported by its manifest.
- `full_protocol_pqcrypto_reported.zip`: full-protocol archive with a native `pqcrypto` backend reported by its manifest.
- `quantum_validation_variant_A.zip` and `quantum_validation_variant_B.zip`: two source archives; their tables may match while plots differ.

No original Kaggle run-history IDs or downloaded-job metadata were included with the supplied ZIPs. Avoid inferring execution provenance solely from archive filenames or embedded manifests.
